      About

      aLogs: Aniiloger's Companion App — an unofficial community companion for Aniimo. Not affiliated with Aniimo or its developers.

      The meter reads the game (its picture, its sound during boss fights, its text log and its data files) and, if you let it, clicks View Battle Data for you. It never changes the game's memory or files.

      How aLogs sees the game on Windows 10. Windows 11 lets aLogs capture just the game window. Windows 10 only allows that with a yellow border drawn round the game, so you choose (General Settings › Capture only the game window):

      • Off (the default): the whole screen the game is on, no border. aLogs' own overlays are kept out of the picture, but anything another program draws over the game is in it: the Discord, Steam or NVIDIA overlay, Windows notifications, or a window on top. Those can end up in your proof pictures and can get in the way of what the meter reads.
      • On: only the game window, with the yellow border. Nothing from other programs gets in. The border shows only while aLogs is capturing (while the meter, leaderboards or Sanctum Helper need the game's picture) and goes when they stop.

      While you're tabbed out with it off, aLogs copies the game window itself a few times a second instead (not while the game is minimized). Where a Sanctum Helper map sits on what it reads (the game's minimap, or labels on the in-game map), Sanctum Helper reads that part without the map in it.

      Updates

      aLogs always checks for updates and shows them in the sidebar. Nothing installs until you choose: Install now closes aLogs, shows a progress bar while it installs and opens the new version; Next time aLogs opens installs it then. No installer to click through either way.

      Report a problem

      Sends your logs to the AniiLogs developers, one report for the whole app: the meter's log, your 20 newest fights' readings, your profile notes, a few small pictures the meter kept of the boss bar and the top of the screen, Sanctum Helper's log and both modules' settings (never your sign-in), with your game UID, the time, which build you have and any screenshots you add. They're kept privately and cleared once the problem is fixed (after 30 days at most).

      Screenshots (optional)

      Up to 5, or paste one with Ctrl+V (a Win+Shift+S snip works). They're sent as they are, so crop out anything private.
