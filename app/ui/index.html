Checking controller blocking…

Press a button on a controller: its row lights up, so you can tell which one is which.

    The meter always keeps your records: each fight's readings, results, Battle Data numbers, your scores (from Challenge Stats and the battle prep screens) and meter.log. The diagnostics below save extra pictures to check the meter's accuracy and fix problems; they're off unless you're chasing a problem, and they take a lot of space.

    The meter reads each screen's numbers into your records (scores, results, Battle Data) and only keeps a picture when it's needed: as proof for the leaderboards, until it has been sent. Anything left over is cleared every hour. Your records are kept either way.

    Send logs

    Something wrong with aLogs? Send your logs to the AniiLogs developers, one report for the whole app: the meter's log, your 20 newest fights' readings, your profile notes, Sanctum Helper's log and both modules' settings (never your sign-in), with your game UID, the time and which build you have. They're kept privately and cleared once the problem is fixed (after 30 days at most).

    Optional. Send your records to the AniiLogs leaderboards (Holo-Battle Interlink and Sim). They show under your in-game name, linked to your AniiLogs account (you sign in with Discord). Nothing is sent unless you turn this on.

    Updates

    aLogs always checks for updates and shows them in the sidebar. Nothing installs until you choose: Install now closes aLogs, shows a progress bar while it installs and opens the new version; Next time aLogs opens installs it then. No installer to click through either way.

    aLogs: Aniiloger's Companion App — an unofficial community companion for Aniimo. Not affiliated with Aniimo or its developers.
